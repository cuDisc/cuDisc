Citation

Third-party methods
-------------------

If your work uses specific numerical components, please also consider citing
the relevant algorithmic papers:

- **DSHARP opacities**: Birnstiel et al. (2018), ApJL 869, L45.
  https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/aaf743
- **Coagulation kernel / DustPy**: Stammler & Birnstiel (2022), ApJ 935, 35.
  https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ac7d58
- **Bogacki–Shampine integrator**: Bogacki & Shampine (1989), Applied
  Mathematics Letters 2(4), 321–325.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/0893-9659(89)90079-7
- **Kley FLD limiter**: Kley (1989), A&A 208, 98.

Publications using cuDisc

The following papers have used cuDisc in their analysis:

- Robinson, Owen & Booth (2026) — CO snow-line structure, volatile transport.
